# BeKampus

BeKampus is a web platform for discovering and participating in university life, starting with the National University of Colombia (UNAL), Bogotá campus.

It brings communities, student groups, events, and announcements into one place. Users can discover public content, follow communities that interest them, receive updates from those communities, and register for eligible events. Organizers can manage community profiles and publish events or announcements, while administrators review applications and moderate content.

Events may be restricted to UNAL students or another defined group, open to students from other institutions, or available to the general public. These access conditions are shown explicitly. Approval on BeKampus does not imply official endorsement by the University.

The initial release is intentionally focused on UNAL Bogotá. Support for other universities is a future direction, not part of the initial MVP.
